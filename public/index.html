Building Damage Assessment Platform

A digital, standardized solution that helps field engineers assess partially damaged buildings in Gaza, document damage with photos and video, and generate structured reports — even without an internet connection.

Start Assessing Learn more

Why this platform?

After the 2023 destruction in Gaza, thousands of buildings need evaluation. Today these assessments are done manually, are not standardized, and are hard to document and share.

  • Replaces slow, paper-based methods
  • Standardized, structured damage records
  • Works in areas with poor connectivity
  • Faster reporting and decision-making
Who is it for?
Field Engineers
Enter building & owner data, assess damage, upload media, generate reports.
Humanitarian Organizations
Collect reports, analyze conditions, prioritize emergency response.
Government Authorities
Use structured records for reconstruction planning.

Core Features

Field Inspection Form

Capture owner info, building details, damage type and severity, and engineer notes in one structured form.

Media Documentation

Attach images and videos of the damage as visual evidence for every assessment.

Automated Reports

Generate a structured PDF report instantly — ready to print, export and share.

Offline Collection

Keep working without internet. Data and photos are stored on the device.

Automatic Sync

When connectivity returns, offline records sync to the central server automatically.

Dashboard & History

Track all your inspections, filter by severity, and review past assessments.
